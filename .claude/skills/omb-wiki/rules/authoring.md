# OpenWiki authoring boundary

The active authoring route is `Skill("omb-wiki")`, which loads the official
installed OpenWiki host skill. Follow its sequential main-host page jobs.
Official OpenWiki owns Claims, indexes, provenance and finalization; OMB never
maintains an independent schema-selected candidate publisher.

Write current facts from actual sources and cite complete evidence locators.
Verify source spans support each Claim, including after line movement.
Preserve identifiers, qualifications and historical decision scope.
Use project `openwiki/INSTRUCTIONS.md` for project-specific authoring policy.

The remaining legacy schemas, templates and validation helpers in this bundle
exist only to inspect or recover pre-migration operations. They do not govern
native OpenWiki pages and must not be selected for new writing.

Read-only review runs after finalization or against a plan, outside the official
page loop. Apply `.claude/rules/common/sot-authoring.md` to durable knowledge.
The frozen migration corpus exception there preserves historical bytes without
making archives current policy or active context.
