Boilerplate (100 words). Sainted Word Records is a one-person audio-reactive video studio based in Belgrade, Serbia, run by Kai Djuric. The flagship product is a browser-native, MIT-licensed engine that turns a song + a library of clips into a layered music video in under a minute — no install, no SaaS, no upload. The engine analyses the audio (BPM, key, beats, chromagram) and renders a real-time composition with 23 personas, 14 WebGL FX, and 20 stackable CSS filters. Output is WebM at 1080p. The engine is self-evolving: a daily cron produces 2 new preset specs that ship to the live app, and after 3 uses of a single persona the engine itself generates a personalized variant tuned to the song's actual BPM and key. The project is open source, local-first, and explicitly not VC-funded.
