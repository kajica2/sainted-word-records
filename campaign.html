10 · Read this before you sell
⚠ License clarity

Your MIT license doesn't cover the bundled media.

The SWR source is MIT-licensed, but the bundled visual library (stock footage, images, video clips) is not. Before you sell a finished video to a customer, verify every asset in the rendered output allows commercial use and redistribution in the final MP4. Also make clear that customers must own or have permission to use the audio they submit — the engine encodes whatever you feed it, and that's their responsibility, not yours. Finally, your own docs say modern Chrome or Safari with a wide window — don't promise identical output on every browser and device.

12 · The questions you'll be asked

Frequently asked.

Do I need to own the audio?

Yes. You must own or have permission to use whatever you submit. SWR encodes whatever audio you feed it — that's your responsibility, not mine. If you used uncleared samples in your track, that's between you and the original rightsholder.

What about the visual library inside the tool?

The SWR source is MIT-licensed, but the bundled visual library is not. I verify every asset in the rendered output before delivery. If you need a fully cleared library for a high-stakes release, ask up front and I'll either swap to cleared assets or source new ones.

Can I post the visualizer on Spotify Canvas?

Yes — the MP4 is H.264 + AAC, 48kHz stereo, 30fps. Standard Spotify Canvas specs are 9:16 vertical between 3–30s. The 30s Standard tier is exactly that.

What browsers and devices does the output play on?

Anywhere H.264 MP4 plays — which is everywhere. Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn, Spotify Canvas, native iOS and Android. Rendering is done in modern Chrome or Safari with a wide browser window; the output file is platform-agnostic.

Do you offer refunds?

Two free revision rounds are included in every tier. If you're not happy with the result after the second revision, full refund — no questions, no friction. Better to lose €25 than to lose a customer and a portfolio piece.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, for Pro and Monthly tiers. Standard and Basic tiers are public promotional content; that's part of the price difference. For label work or unreleased music, expect NDA + custom scope + a slightly higher rate.
