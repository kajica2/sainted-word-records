# Marketing Scripts

Four 30-second spoken scripts, one per audience, for Sainted Word Records.

## What's here

| File | Audience | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `01-music-creators.md` | Artists, producers, beatmakers, songwriters | You're talking to someone who makes music and wants visuals for it. The pitch leans on "reacts to your actual song, not templates." |
| `02-video-creators.md` | YouTubers, TikTokers, short-form editors | You're talking to someone drowning in raw clips who hates After Effects. The pitch leans on "your existing folder suddenly feels alive." |
| `03-educators.md` | Teachers, lecturers, course creators | You're talking to someone who cares about retention and engagement, not "cool visuals." The pitch leans on "your lecture finally has a pulse." |
| `04-casual-creators.md` | Birthday-video, Instagram-story, DJ-set-opener crowd | You're talking to someone non-technical who came for a vibe. Zero jargon, plain "you" language, no acronyms. |

## How to read them

Each file follows the same shape, in order:

1. **Hook** — first sentence, has to earn the next 3 seconds.
2. **Problem** — one sentence about what sucks today.
3. **Pivot** — the "but here's the thing" beat.
4. **Value prop** — 1–2 sentences on what SWR actually does.
5. **Demo moment** — one sentence showing what it looks like.
6. **Close** — CTA: free, no signup, the URL.
7. **Pacing notes in brackets** — `[beat]`, `[pause]`, `[lean in]`, `[wink]`, etc. for the speaker.

Pacing notes are direction, not choreography. Drop the ones that don't fit your read.

## A meta-note about the brand voice

This brand should **never** sound like a press release.

- No "we're excited to announce."
- No "revolutionize," "unleash," "elevate," "unlock," "empower," "cutting-edge," "game-changing," "ecosystem," "synergy."
- No "AI-powered" — it does use AI (BPM, key, chromagram analysis), but lead with what it does, not what's inside.
- No "seamless" or "robust." If you can't describe it in plain words, the product isn't ready to be described.

The product was built by a working jazz saxophonist in Belgrade, not a marketing team. That fact is a feature. Use it. A musician explaining visuals to another musician is the entire brand voice in one sentence — if your copy could have come from any SaaS landing page, throw it out and rewrite.

The product is also **free in the browser with no signup**. Lead with that every time. Don't bury it behind pricing or features.

## Constraints baked into these scripts

- 60–90 words per script (~30 seconds at normal speaking pace).
- Plain Markdown, no frontmatter.
- No emoji in the scripts themselves.
- No promises about features you don't know exist — these scripts only claim: audio-reactive visuals, drop in song + library, browser-native, free, no signup.
