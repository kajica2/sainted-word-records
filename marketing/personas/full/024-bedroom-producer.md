# The Bedroom Producer

**Slug:** bedroom-producer
**Surfaces touched (3+ minimum):** engine, presets, library, recorder, versions
**One-line:** A SoundCloud bedroom producer who picks an anchor per track, stacks two loop stills from the library, and records a 30-second WebM for the SoundCloud visual.

## Who they are
You are a bedroom producer in your early twenties who has been putting tracks on SoundCloud for three years and who has started adding visuals to your tracks because the platform now surfaces them. You are not a motion designer and you have never opened After Effects. Your toolbelt is Ableton, a folder of loops you have chopped, and a willingness to learn one new tool per quarter if it helps the streams.

## What you're trying to do
You want to drop your latest track into /engine, lock the variant via the anchor map (`glitch` for the loud song, `phosphor` for the ambient one, `kraft` for the ballad), stack two loop stills from the library behind the track, and record a 30-second WebM via MediaRecorder. You upload the WebM to SoundCloud as the track's visual.

## Which surfaces they actually use, and why
1. **/engine** — the compositor where you stack the track and the two loop stills.
2. **Presets (anchor map)** — you assign one of the 19 anchors per track; `glitch`, `phosphor`, and `kraft` are your usual three.
3. **Library** — you pull two loop stills from the library per track.
4. **Recorder** — you record a 30-second WebM per track for SoundCloud.
5. **Versions** — you lock the variant per track via the version pages.

## A typical session (90-180 minutes)
1. You drop your latest track into /engine, audio-analysis v2 returns BPM=140, key=F# minor.
2. You lock /versions/glitch.html, push `chroma=0.6`, `grain=0.5` (cold, fractured).
3. You stack two loop stills from the library: `loop-city-traffic.mp4`, `loop-rain-window.mp4`.
4. You record a 30-second WebM via MediaRecorder.
5. You upload the WebM to SoundCloud as the track's visual.
6. You almost quit at step 3 — the library's first search result for "city" was a still, not a loop. You almost gave up before scrolling to the loop.

## What they'd pay for
You would not pay — you are a bedroom producer and your budget is the streaming revenue. You would happily accept a free "indie tier" with longer WebM capture (60 seconds instead of 30) so you can author 60-second hooks for Twitter.

## What would make them leave
You would leave forever if MediaRecorder dropped WebM support, because SoundCloud's upload pipeline assumes WebM input. You would tolerate the 30-second record limit forever — it is enough for a SoundCloud visual.

## Quote
"The /versions/glitch.html variant on my track and the MediaRecorder WebM are what SoundCloud surfaces, which is why the engine replaced the After Effects tutorials I was watching."
