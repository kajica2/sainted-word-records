# Sample Pack Producer

**Slug:** sample-pack-producer
**One-line:** A bedroom producer selling loop kits on Gumroad or BeatStars who needs audio-reactive preview GIFs for every pack.

## Who they are

You are 24 to 38, you make and sell sample packs — drum kits, melodic loops, one-shots — through your own Shopify or via marketplaces like BeatStars and Loopmasters. You saw a Sainted Word Records link dropped in a producer forum where someone complained about placeholder preview GIFs. Your day is making the actual product, making the preview, marketing, and trying to grow a mailing list. Your toolbelt is your DAW, a sample-editor plugin, a screen-recorder, and a Squarespace site that is overdue for a redesign.

## What they're trying to do

You want each loop in your pack to have a preview that pulses and breathes, so a buyer scrolling your BeatStars page sees something alive — a 6-to-10 second reactive GIF that fits the energy of the loop without rendering in After Effects. Success looks like a pack listing with one hundred audio-reactive previews that load fast on a phone and that buyers describe as "felt the vibe immediately."

## Why they don't use the alternatives

You tried AE4X for batch rendering previews, but you cannot find a way to loop the audio-react across every sample without scripting that you would have to write yourself. You have used Splice's in-house preview tool and it works fine, but you want a unique look for your own site. Sainted Word Records lets you drop in your audio plus a few of your own stills and react it, batch-style, without scripting.

## Their first session

1. You open Sainted Word Records on a Sunday between freelance work and dinner.
2. You drop in your latest drum-kit demo track and the engine reports a BPM of 92 — close to what your DAW says, which is fine for a hip-hop kit.
3. You drag in a folder of branded stills from your website — your logo, your color palette PNGs, the cover art for last month's pack.
4. You pick the `pulse` style because it reads cleanly at small sizes.
5. You hit export, get a WebM, and convert it to a GIF with a tool you already use for marketing assets.
6. You upload the GIF to your BeatStars listing and watch your conversion rate climb 8% the next week — you almost do not believe the analytics.

## What they'd pay for

You would pay ten to twenty dollars a month for a marketplace that connects your packs to people searching "audio-reactive loops," because right now you pay BeatStars a fee on every sale and that is your only distribution. You would not pay for a base subscription that adds watermarks or removes features.

## What would make them leave

You would leave forever if Sainted Word Records ever showed another producer's branding on a pack you were exporting — that is your revenue, and you would not risk the trademark issue. You would tolerate a five-minute wait for high-volume batch exports, because you already batch.

## Quote

"My preview GIFs stopped looking like everyone else's preview GIFs, and that is worth ten dollars a month to me."
