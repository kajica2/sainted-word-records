# TikTok & influencer offers — daily reactive video arcs

**Program name (working):** **Daily Arc** — one new 9:16 audio-reactive clip per day, on a visual theme that matches the creator’s channel and **grows** each day (more layers, stronger grade, bolder motion, longer cut).

**What they get:** Clips that **react to real audio** (their track, voice, or a clip you agree on) — not a static template. Built with SWR (`/engine`, `/tiktok`, or done-for-you render).

**What you ask:** Credit, one post or stitch per week (tier-dependent), and rights to repost the best clip on SWR social with their handle.

**Voice:** Plain language. No “AI-powered,” “revolutionize,” or “seamless.” Lead with **free in the browser** when the offer is self-serve.

---

## How the daily arc works

Each creator gets a **Channel Theme Pack**: colors, mood, and look family locked to their niche. Each **calendar day** adds one step on an escalation ladder so followers feel the channel “leveling up.”

| Day | Arc name | Visual expansion | Typical length | Posting hook (caption starter) |
|-----|----------|------------------|----------------|--------------------------------|
| **1** | **Spark** | Single-layer reactive field, light grade (`film` or `neon`) | 8–12s | “Day 1 — the beat found a picture.” |
| **2** | **Stack** | 2 layers, same palette, subtle FX (`grain`, `vignette`) | 10–15s | “Day 2 — added a second layer. Same song, more depth.” |
| **3** | **Pulse** | Beat-linked scale/opacity; transition on first chorus (`whip-blur` or `circle-wipe`) | 15–20s | “Day 3 — it moves on the kick now.” |
| **4** | **Shift** | Swap or blend **variant look** (`versions/` — e.g. neon → aurora) | 15–25s | “Day 4 — new look, same audio.” |
| **5** | **Storm** | Higher FX intensity + 3+ layers or grouped stacks | 20–30s | “Day 5 — turned the reactors up.” |
| **6** | **Brand** | Their colors / logo / handle burn-in (safe zone for TikTok UI) | 20–35s | “Day 6 — this one’s mine.” |
| **7** | **Finale** | Full composition + best transition + export for **full hook length** they use (15s / 30s / 60s) | 30–60s | “Week 1 complete — which day hit hardest?” |

**After day 7:** **Month arc** resets palette but keeps escalation rules — e.g. week 2 introduces **live mic/camera layer** (`/engine` Live) or **new song** per day for producers.

**Technical note for fulfillment:** Day-to-day “expansion” is authored by increasing layer count, `FX.intensity`, preset warmth, and transition vocabulary — not by changing the underlying song without agreement.

## Channel Theme Packs (map niche → SWR looks)

Use these as defaults when briefing fulfillment. Adjust after you see their feed.

| Niche | Palette / mood | Primary looks (`/versions` or engine) | Transition bias |
|-------|----------------|--------------------------------------|-----------------|
| Trap / hip-hop | High contrast, neon | `neon`, `glitch`, `chrome` | `glitch-block`, `snap-zoom` |
| House / techno | Grid, brutal | `grid`, `spectrum`, `phosphor` | `whip-blur`, `chromatic-split` |
| Indie / sad | Warm, film | `film`, `smoke`, `tape` | `pure-crossfade`, `lens-flare` |
| Ambient / study | Soft, slow | `watercolor`, `aurora`, `void` | `fade-to-black`, slow crossfade |
| DJ / club | Aggressive peaks | `hallucination`, `pulse`, `music_video` | `flash-cover`, `chromatic-split` |
| Podcast / talk | Clean, readable | `film`, `grid` + strong captions | minimal transitions |
| Dance | Sharp motion | `neon`, `pulse` | `whip-blur`, `swivel` |
| Jazz / live | Organic | `film`, `baroque`, `collage` | subtle, manual only |
