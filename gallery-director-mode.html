§ 3 · How to use this page

A catalogue, not a sales pitch.

The engine's variants stay as peers — no theme picker collapses them into one dropdown. This page reflects that.

step 01

Open the engine

Drop a track into /engine. Audio analysis v2 returns BPM, key, scale, and a 12-bin chromagram in real time.

step 02

Hover the anchor map

The 19 anchor presets plot on a (warmth × intensity) grid. Click a dot to load that variant. The chromagram weight reads as the visual palette.

step 03

Push FX by hand

14 uniforms — chroma, grain, glow, temp, sepia, bloom, vignette, blur, posterize, mut, mutAlgo, grayscale, liquid, pearl. No auto-correct pass.

step 04

Record as WebM

MediaRecorder captures the canvas as .webm. Drop into /ar-loop for markerless AR, or transcode to MP4 with ffmpeg locally.
