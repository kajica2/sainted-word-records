# Code of Conduct

## Short version

Be kind. Accept feedback gracefully. Ship working code over clever
code. Help others. Don't be a jerk.

## The long version

We expect every contributor, maintainer, and visitor to behave
professionally. That doesn't mean humourless — it means treating
people like you'd want to be treated. Specifically:

### Encouraged

- Welcoming new contributors — first contributions are scary.
- Explaining *why*, not just *what*. Code reviewers coach, they don't gatekeep.
- Saying "I don't know" when you don't.
- Asking for help. Public channels are public; private DMs for
  sensitive questions only.
- Calling out behaviour that isn't welcoming, in the moment when
  it's natural to do so.

### Discouraged

- Harassment, in any form — verbal, written, or via imagery.
- Trolling, flaming, sustained off-topic.
- Personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.

### Enforcement

Reports go to the maintainers (see `SECURITY.md` for the disclosure
channel). All complaints are reviewed and investigated, and result in
a response that is proportionate to the circumstances. Maintainers
who don't follow the policy themselves face temporary or permanent
removal from the project team.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant
v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
and the spirit of the [Mozilla Manifesto](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/about/manifesto/details/).
