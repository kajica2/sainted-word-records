How it works

Make Video is the quick path from a song file and a folder of clips to a downloadable video — all in one tab.

  1. Pick your song

    Choose an audio file from your device. It stays in your browser storage for this session — not on our servers.

  2. Add clips

    Import videos and stills into the clip library, then place them on the timeline in the order you want.

  3. Preview & react

    Play the timeline on the canvas. Toggle the built-in visualizer for spectrum bars, waveform, and beat pulse over your edit.

  4. Record & download

    Pick an aspect ratio, press record, and save MP4 (or WebM if your browser requires it). No watermark is added in this tool.

FAQ

  • Does it upload my song or clips?

    No. Make Video stores media in your browser (IndexedDB) and plays it locally. We do not receive your audio or video files for editing.

  • Is the export watermarked?

    Make Video records the preview canvas directly and does not add a watermark layer in that tool. Other pages on the site may use different export rules.

  • MP4 or WebM?

    The recorder tries MP4 first (video/mp4 with H.264/AAC when supported). If your browser cannot encode MP4, it falls back to WebM and still downloads locally.

  • Can it auto-cut my footage to the beat?

    Not in the Make Video timeline itself — you arrange clips there. Beat-synced layer routing and automix live in the main engine; Auto-pick style is the bridge from Make Video into that workflow.

  • Is it really free?

    Yes. The tool runs in your browser with no subscription. Optional paid services on the site (renders, licenses) are separate and never required to use Make Video.

  • What audio files work?

    Any format your browser can decode (typically MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC). Analysis for BPM and key uses the same in-browser pipeline as the rest of the engine.
