Quick start

  1. Drop a song file anywhere — the audio becomes the score.
  2. Drop a library of clips / GIFs / images — the visualizer reads them.
  3. Hit Play — bass, mids, treble, beats drive the composition.
  4. Hit 🎬 Export — the whole song renders to a video file.
