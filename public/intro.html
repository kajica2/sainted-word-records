Sainted Word Records · 90-second intro

Drop a song. Drop a library. Get a music video.

Sainted Word Records is a browser-based audio-reactive video engine. The engine reads your song, scores every clip in your library by how it actually moves, and composes a layered real-time video that breathes with the music. Export as MP4. Local-first. MIT-licensed. No account.

How it works · 3 steps

From audio file to MP4 in one tab.

01 · LOAD

Drop a song and a library of clips.

MP3, WAV, M4A. Drag any number of MP4 / WebM / GIF / PNG / JPG files onto the page. The engine scores each by motion, luma, and hue — not by filename.

02 · PLAY

Press play. The audio drives the visuals.

Web Audio analyser extracts BPM, key, beats, and chromagram in real time. The 6 layers mix, blend, and pulse with the song. Re-map any time for a fresh look.

03 · REC

Record 10s, 30s, 60s, or full song.

One click exports native H.264 + AAC MP4 at 1080p, 8 Mbps. Lands in your downloads folder. Plays in any video editor or straight to social.

Who it's for

If you have audio and need video, this is for you.

Independent musicians

You have the track. You need a YouTube visualizer, an IG Reel, a Bandcamp loop. Hiring an editor is €300+ and 1–3 weeks. This is €0 and one afternoon.

Producers & beatmakers

Sketch visual ideas for unreleased tracks without leaving the browser. The engine reads BPM and key, so the visual mood matches the track's actual emotion.

VJs & live performers

Use your own performance footage. The engine scores by motion, so a clip of a person dancing ranks higher than a still. Plug in a mic, layer live video over the song.

Designers prototyping motion

Need a 10-second mood piece for a brief? The engine is a fast way to test visual languages against an audio reference. Export, drop into a deck, iterate.
