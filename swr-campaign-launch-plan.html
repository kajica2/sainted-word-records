The Product Reframe

Instead of selling "an engine" or "a video," sell curated media sets — pre-selected bundles of clips, stills, and visual treatments that guarantee a specific aesthetic outcome. The customer doesn't pick individual assets. They pick a vibe, drop their song, and receive a finished MP4.

This is the difference between:

  • ❌ "Upload your own clips to our tool"
  • ✅ "The 'Neon Church' set — 8 assets, 2 video loops, 6 stills, electric magenta treatment, €35"
