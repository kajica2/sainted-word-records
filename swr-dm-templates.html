DM & email outreach templates

Eight copy-paste templates for initial outreach, follow-ups, genre pitches, and label contacts.

1. Initial Cold DM

Short Version

Hey [artist name] — I came across your track ["song name"] and it hit just right. I make video visuals for artists in the [genre] space, and I think your sound would fit [set name] perfectly. Want a free 10-second preview with your track? No strings attached — just curious if the vibe lands.

Slightly Warmer Version (if mutuals / local)

[artist name] — been following your stuff for a minute. "song name" is solid. I run SWR Visualizers, making video sets for [genre] artists. [set name] feels like it was built around your sound. Happy to throw together a free 10s preview so you can see what it looks like with your track. Let me know.

2. Follow-up DM (5 Days After Initial)

Hey [artist name] — no pressure, just circling back. I know DMs pile up. Still happy to send that free 10s preview of [set name] with your track if you're curious. If it's not the right time, all good — just wanted to make sure it didn't get buried.

4. Portfolio Request Response

("What does this look like?")

Here's a quick clip of [set name] in action: [link to portfolio clip]. That one was done for [reference artist, or "a producer in the [genre] space" if confidential]. I can also send a 10s preview using your own track so you see exactly how your sound translates — no charge, just want you to know what you're getting.

5. Pricing Objection Response

("€25 is too much")

Totally get it — every artist's budget is different right now. Just to be transparent: €25 is for a full 4-minute render, not a loop, and you're free to use it however you want (YT, live shows, promo). That said, I do run a founding customer deal — 30% off if you're open to me using a 10s clip in my portfolio. Brings it down to €17.50. No pressure either way.

6. Founding Customer Discount Pitch

Hey [artist name] — I'm building out the public portfolio for SWR and looking for a handful of artists whose work I genuinely want to feature. If ["song name"] or another track feels like a fit, I'm offering 30% off any tier in exchange for letting me use a 10-second clip in my portfolio. Brings a single set down to €17.50, double to €31.50, the full pack to €52.50. Only taking on a few — let me know if you're interested.

7. Local / Belgrade Artist Pitch

[artist name] — Belgrade's [genre] scene is small enough that good work actually gets noticed. I run SWR Visualizers from here and I'm trying to build something that reps local sound properly. [set name] feels like it matches what you're doing. Happy to send a free 10s preview with your track, see if the vibe clicks.

8. Label Outreach (Formal, B2B)

Subject: Visual sets for [label name] artists — SWR Visualizers

Hi [contact name],

I run SWR Visualizers, a service producing curated video visual sets for music artists. I came across [label name] through [artist or release] and wanted to reach out — I think the [set name] set would be a strong visual fit for artists in your roster working in the [genre] space.

We offer single-set renders at €25, multi-set packages up to €75 for the full collection, and custom source integration at €55. Renderings are delivery-ready for YouTube, social cuts, and live performance backdrops. Happy to provide a portfolio reel or a sample clip synced to one of your releases on request.

Let me know if a quick call or email exchange works — no pressure, just wanted to put us on your radar.

Best, [your name] SWR Visualizers [website or portfolio link] [email]

Quick Reference: Placeholders

Placeholder Meaning
[artist name] Recipient's artist or real name
["song name"] Specific track you're referencing
[genre] Their genre (techno, indie folk, etc.)
[set name] Matched visual set (see 5 sets above)
[link to portfolio clip] URL to example work
[reference artist] Past client or public example
[label name] Target label
[contact name] Label A&R or manager name
[your name] Your name
[website or portfolio link] SWR site or reel
[email] Your contact email
