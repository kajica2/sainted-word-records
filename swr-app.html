How it works

Three steps from sound to screen.

No plugins. No DAW. No render farm. Just a browser tab and a track.

01 / Upload

Drop in audio.

Load an MP3, WAV, or FLAC — or pipe in the microphone for live input. Bass, mids, and treble activity are extracted and start driving the visuals immediately.

02 / Shape

Tune the visual system.

Choose a preset, scrub the reactive parameters, stack layers of typography, overlays and effects. Every slider you touch is visible on the preview in real time.

03 / Record

Render and share.

Pick a 9:16, 1:1, or 16:9 output. Hit record. The engine captures a video synced to the audio, ready for socials, sets, and portfolios.

First time here

Load your media to get started.

Drop in songs (mp3, wav), video clips (mp4, mpg), or stills (png, gif, jpg). The engine will route each to the right place — audio drives the visualizer, video + images fill your media library. You can skip this and use the Media tab anytime.

  • audiomp3 · wav · ogg · m4a · flac
  • videomp4 · mpg · webm · mov
  • imagepng · jpg · gif · webp · avif
