What I do

Three services. They overlap more than you'd expect — a transcribed chart usually wants a rehearsal recording, and a music video usually wants a mastered master.

01

Transcription & engraving

Charts that a band can actually read — parts in the right order, correct transpositions, and rehearsal audio so the drummer learns it before the first downbeat.

  • From audio, or from a MIDI transcription
  • Full-score PDF + per-part PDFs
  • Multi-track MIDI and a rehearsal mix
  • Muted backing versions (no trumpet / no sax)

How it goes

  1. You send the source. A recording, a MIDI file, a reference track, or two sentences and a humming — whichever you have.
  2. You get a scope. What's included, what it costs, how long, and how many revisions. Fixed before any work starts.
  3. You get a first pass. Enough to judge the direction, not a finished thing you'll feel obliged to approve.
  4. Files land. Everything in the deliverables list above, in your format, with the editable sources.
