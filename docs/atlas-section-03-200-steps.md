# Section III — The 200 Steps

**The Core Curriculum**

Four phases of 50 steps each. The phases are not strictly sequential — you can be working on a Phase 3 step while still practicing a Phase 1 anchor. But there is a general direction: soil before training, training before unraveling, unraveling before radiating.

---

## Phase 1: Cultivating the Soil (Steps 1–50)

*The foundation work. Build the container that will hold everything else.*

| Step | Practice | Anchor |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Radical honesty | Tell the truth about one small thing today. |
| 2 | Self-compassion | Speak to yourself the way you would speak to a child. |
| 3 | Daily meditation | Sit for 20 minutes. Focus on the breath. |
| 4 | Sleep hygiene | Same bedtime, same wake time, every day for a week. |
| 5 | Hydration | One liter of water before noon. |
| 6 | Walking | 20 minutes of bilateral movement. |
| 7 | One thing at a time | Pick one task. Close everything else. |
| 8 | Saying no | Decline one request today that you would have said yes to. |
| 9 | Reading | 20 pages of something that isn't news. |
| 10 | Setting an intention | Decide the *one* thing today is for. |
| ... | ... | ... |

*(Steps 11–50 follow the same pattern: small, daily, repeatable practices that establish the soil. Full list: appendices forthcoming.)*

The principle: **you cannot build on what you haven't leveled.** Phase 1 is leveling. Don't skip it because it feels too simple. The simplicity is the point.

---

## Phase 2: Training the Mind (Steps 51–100)

*Once the soil is established, the mind becomes the practice.*

| Step | Practice | Anchor |
|---|---|---|
| 51 | Vipassana | Observe the changing nature of thoughts without grasping. |
| 52 | Equanimity | Stay balanced when circumstances change. |
| 53 | Non-attachment | Let go of one specific expectation for an outcome. |
| 54 | Witness practice | Watch the mind think without participating. |
| 55 | Noting | Label experience as it arises: "sound, sound, thought, sensation." |
| 56 | Choice-point awareness | Notice the moment before a reaction. |
| 57 | Energy hygiene | Audit what you consume in a day — input is also practice. |
| 58 | Gratitude without comparison | Be grateful without ranking the gifts. |
| 59 | Difficult conversations | Have one you've been avoiding. |
| 60 | Repair | Apologize for one thing you've let sit. |
| ... | ... | ... |

*(Steps 61–100 develop the capacity to be present to experience without being consumed by it.)*

The principle: **the mind is a tool, not an identity.** Phase 2 builds the ability to use the mind without being used by it.

---

## Phase 3: Unraveling the Illusion (Steps 101–150)

*The turning point. What was built in Phase 1 is gently dismantled in Phase 3.*

| Step | Practice | Anchor |
|---|---|---|
| 101 | The Three Marks | Anicca (impermanence), Dukkha (suffering), Anatta (non-self). |
| 102 | Emptying | Recognize the interconnectedness of all things. |
| 103 | Self-inquiry | Constantly ask: "Who is the 'I' that is experiencing this?" |
| 104 | Death contemplation | Sit with the certainty of your own ending. |
| 105 | The witness and the witnessed | Notice that the observer is also observed. |
| 106 | Letting go of views | Hold a position lightly. Be ready to revise it. |
| 107 | The ungraspable mind | Thoughts arise without a thinker behind them. |
| 108 | Direct pointing | What is awareness right now, before any concept of it? |
| 109 | Suffering as teacher | Treat pain as information, not as punishment. |
| 110 | Identity experiments | Notice which "selves" you defend. Try not defending them. |
| ... | ... | ... |

*(Steps 111–150 deepen the dismantling begun in 101–110.)*

The principle: **the self you built is not the self you are.** Phase 3 is the careful deconstruction of that misconception. It is not nihilism. It is precision.

---

## Phase 4: Radiating Awareness (Steps 151–200)

*What remains when the illusion is seen through is not nothing. It is everything, recognized.*

| Step | Practice | Anchor |
|---|---|---|
| 151 | Service | Dedicate your life to the benefit of all beings. |
| 152 | Awe | Maintain a Beginner's Mind toward the universe. |
| 153 | Unity | Live in the realization that there is no "other." |
| 154 | Compassion without boundary | Extend care beyond your in-group. |
| 155 | Forgiveness as practice | Forgive the unforgivable — not for them, for you. |
| 156 | Generosity as identity | Give without tracking the return. |
| 157 | Work as offering | Whatever your hands find to do, do it as practice. |
| 158 | Spaciousness | Make room for others to fail, grow, and be. |
| 159 | Unconditional presence | Be fully here for whoever is in front of you. |
| 160 | The smile that precedes content | Let the face settle into ease before the mind settles into meaning. |
| ... | ... | ... |

*(Steps 161–200 bring the practice into the world, not as escape from it but as full engagement with it.)*

The principle: **enlightenment is not a state. It is what is left when you stop preventing it.** Phase 4 is the return to ordinary life, with full awareness.

---

## The sequence is a heuristic, not a law

You will find yourself returning to Phase 1 in your 30s, your 50s, your 70s. Each life-event that disrupts your foundations sends you back to soil-work. This is not regression. This is how the curriculum actually works when lived. Treat the phases as seasons, not semesters.
