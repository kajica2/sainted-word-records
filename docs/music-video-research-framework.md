# Music Video Editing Research Framework

A working research framework for music video analysis, transition design, and
post-production. Adapted from a daily-cron methodology note dated 2026-09-09.

## Integration across research elements

This framework is designed to be adaptable while maintaining rigorous
standards. The key to success is the integration of research elements:

- **Analysis informs Practice** — each video analyzed provides techniques to implement
- **Practice validates Research** — implementation confirms or challenges findings
- **Technology expands Possibilities** — new tools enable new transition types
- **Awards set Benchmarks** — award-winning work establishes quality standards
- **Genres provide Context** — understanding genres informs technique selection
- **Psychology explains Impact** — understanding viewer response guides creative decisions
- **History provides Perspective** — knowing evolution shows what works long-term
- **Best Practices ensure Quality** — guidelines maintain consistent professional standards
- **Professional Development tracks Growth** — metrics show progress and identify gaps

The ultimate goal is not just knowledge accumulation, but the development of
an intuitive, musical, and technically proficient approach to music video
editing that can adapt to changing trends and technologies while always
serving the music and artist effectively.

## Picking the right transition

Mood-to-technique mapping from the standard transition toolkit:

| Mood | Reach for |
|---|---|
| Pop chorus, hype | Whip Pan, Lens Flare, Strobe |
| Hip-hop, trap | Glitch, Chromatic, Strobe |
| Indie / alt, emotional | Paint Stroke, Circle Wipe, Warp |
| Cinematic, slow | Lens Flare, Warp, Zoom Through |
| Electronic, drop | Glitch, Chromatic, Zoom Through |
| Performance, choreography | Swivel, Whip Pan, Circle Wipe |

## Categories of transition

- **Cover** — Whip Pan, Swivel Clone
- **Distortion** — Glitch Block, Chromatic Split
- **Spatial** — Zoom Through Object
- **Brightness** — Strobe Flash, Lens Flare Sweep
- **Mask** — Paint Stroke, Circle Wipe
- **Hybrid** — Warp + Dissolve
