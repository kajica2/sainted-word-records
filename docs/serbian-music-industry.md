# Serbian Music Industry — Labels & Publishing Landscape

Reference notes for SWR business/pack work. Compiled 2026-09-30. These
companies are the backbone of the Serbian music industry, often combining
record-label operations with music publishing and copyright administration.

## Major Labels / Publishers

**PGP RTS**
Often described as the oldest and largest music publishing house in the
Balkans. Operating for nearly eight decades; a symbol of quality. Has worked
with the best authors across diverse genres — pop, rock, and traditional
music.

**Grand Production**
Founded 1998. Arguably the most famous Serbian music label. Specializes
heavily in Pop-Folk; manages publishing rights for tens of thousands of
songs. Controls the catalogs of some of the biggest stars in the region.
Also produces popular TV shows like *Zvezde Granda*.

**City Records**
Founded 1997. One of the most influential record and publishing houses in
Serbia and the region. Known for publishing pop, folk, and dance music;
collaborates with a large number of popular performers.

**Mascom / Tin Drum Music**
Mascom is a group of companies specializing in music and book publishing.
Its Tin Drum Music division administers music copyrights and represents
major international catalogs in Serbia, including Warner Chappell Music and
Schubert Music Publishing. Mascom Records is an exclusive partner of the
Warner Music Group.

**Multimedia Records**
Established 2002. One of the leading music publishing and distribution
houses in Serbia. For its first eight years it served as the licensed
partner for Universal Music Group in Serbia, distributing thousands of
international releases.

**Metropolis Records**
Founded 1994. Prominent independent record label and music publishing
house. Specializes in alternative rock, punk, reggae, and jazz; serves as
the exclusive representative for Universal Music in Serbia.

## International Representation & Specialized Publishers

**RICoM Publishing**
Licensed partner of Universal Music Publishing (UMPG). Protects the UMPG
catalog for Serbia, Montenegro, Bosnia and Herzegovina, and Macedonia. Also
represents other catalogs like MenArt and Aquarius Publishing.

**Menart Group**
Regional; plays a key role in distributing major global releases in the
Balkans. Part of a regional association of independent labels; represents
the interests of major international companies in the Serbian market.

**Kontra Rights**
Founded 2004. Started as a pop culture magazine, evolved into a record
label and publisher. Focuses on protecting publishing and neighboring rights
for music creators; also operates as a label and management company.

## Collective Rights Management

**SOKOJ** (Organization of Music Authors of Serbia) — not a publishing
company, but the specialized organization for collective management of
copyright for composers, songwriters, and arrangers in Serbia. Almost all
publishers and authors work with SOKOJ to collect royalties.

## Summary

The landscape is led by historic powerhouses (PGP RTS, Grand Production)
alongside influential contemporary players (City Records, Mascom), all
supported by the collective management work of SOKOJ.
