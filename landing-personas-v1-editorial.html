Colophon

Set in Fraunces — a contemporary serif with optical sizes, variable weight, and an italic that leans the right way for magazine work. Kicker labels and small caps are set in Geist Mono. Printed on a warm paper at #f5efe4, with rules in terracotta and a forest-green marker for the two profiles the engine was tuned against first. No sans was introduced; the body sits at a slightly lighter weight of the same serif used for the display.
