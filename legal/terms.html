3. Service tier — "I render for you"

You submit a song + (optionally) a library. The service team produces a 1080p WebM. Three pre-curated media sets, plus a custom-source option.

  • Delivery. 48 hours from payment confirmation, business days only.
  • Rights. You receive a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to the rendered WebM. The media sets themselves remain the studio's IP — you may not resell or redistribute the raw media, but you may use the rendered video in any project (music videos, social posts, performance visuals, etc.).
  • Refunds. If the rendered video is materially different from the brief (different BPM response, missing assets, broken export), we re-render once for free. After that, refunds are at our discretion.
  • Source material. You must have the rights to the song + library you submit. We don't accept copyrighted material without proof of license.

4. Personal Tier (PT) — engine license for yourself

Buying a PT key removes the engine's watermark on exports and gives you a per-tier credit bank for future features (MP4 export, 4K vertical, multi-seat).

  • Format. swr-{tier}-{code}, e.g. swr-band-A1B2C3D4.
  • Tiers.
    • PT Solo · €120 · 50 credits · 1 seat · 1 year of updates
    • PT Band · €280 · 150 credits · 1 seat · 2 years + priority support
    • PT Label · €600 · 500 credits · 5 seats · lifetime updates
  • License scope. Removes the engine's watermark on exports. Credits bank is for future features (v1.1 MP4 export, v1.3 4K vertical). The engine itself is and remains MIT-licensed — your PT key doesn't change the engine's open-source status, it just unlocks features inside the running app.
  • No telemetry. Your key is stored in your localStorage. We don't see your usage.
  • Key format. MVP — the key is not cryptographically signed. Don't expect DRM. (A signed-license + Stripe-webhook flow is on the roadmap for v1.1.)
  • Refunds. Within 14 days of purchase, if the engine doesn't work for you, we'll refund. After 14 days, no refunds on PT keys.
  • Resale. You may not resell your PT key. Tier is per-seat; for a team, buy the Label tier or multiple Solo/Band keys.
