Quick answer

How do I make a Spotify Canvas from my track? Use the SWR engine: open /engine/?canvas=1 (sets 9:16 and an 8-second record timer), load your song, add visuals, press record while the track plays, download the file, then upload it on your track in Spotify for Artists.

Steps: Canvas from your song

  1. Open the engine with the Canvas preset

    Go to the engine with ?canvas=1. That selects Reel / TikTok / Canvas · 1080×1920 and an 8s auto-stop on the record controls (within Spotify’s commonly cited 3–8 second window).

  2. Load your track

    Drop your audio file into the engine. Analysis runs locally so layers can follow beats, onsets, and frequency bands. See the 30-second tutorial if you are new to the transport controls.

  3. Add visuals that move with the music

    Upload short clips, GIFs, or stills, or use the default library. Try a Neon, Film, or Smoke look from the Looks menu so the loop feels alive on a phone screen.

  4. Record an 8-second vertical clip

    Start playback, press REC (or use Export video for a one-click capture at the selected duration). The engine re-fits the stage to 1080×1920 for the export. Prefer a section where the energy matches your single.

  5. Upload to Spotify for Artists

    Download the MP4 (or WebM if your browser did not offer MP4). In Spotify for Artists, open your release, add Canvas, and upload the file. Confirm current specs in Spotify’s help center before going live.

FAQ

What are Spotify Canvas size and length requirements?

Canvas is a short looping vertical video on mobile. Spotify’s public artist guidance describes a portrait clip, commonly summarized as 9:16 and roughly 3 to 8 seconds long, with file type and size limits that can change. Always confirm in Spotify for Artists help before upload.

How is this different from a “Spotify Canvas generator”?

Most generators animate a static cover. Here you compose reactive layers in the browser, driven by your actual track, then record a slice of that performance. You control clips, look, and timing.

Will my loop be seamless?

Spotify loops the file end-to-end. SWR does not add a dedicated “perfect loop” pass. Pick a stable visual moment, or edit the file so the last frame matches the first.

Is the export free? Any watermark?

The engine is free to use in the browser. Recordings include a small SWR watermark. We do not claim “no watermark” on this page; plan for the mark in the corner.

Does Spotify endorse Sainted Word Records?

No. “Spotify” and “Canvas” refer to the feature on the streaming service. SWR is an independent tool; upload through your own Spotify for Artists account.

Can I use unreleased music?

Yes for local preview and export. Your files are not sent to SWR for rendering. Only upload to Spotify when you have rights to distribute the track.
