Temple of Control

Three temperature sliders turn a meditation into a visual. Heat is the colour temperature, breath the motion temperature, glow the light temperature. Drop in a .mid and the score becomes the meditation.

Automap — hardware or keyboard as the desk

A MIDI controller can drive the three temperatures directly.

Keyboard fallback:

  • 1-9, 0 → heat (0.1 → 1.0)
  • Shift+1-9, Shift+0 → breath (0.1 → 1.0)
  • Ctrl+1-9, Ctrl+0 → glow (0.1 → 1.0)
  • M → cycle mode

Plug it into any page

Two files, no build step. The element is a Web Component.

<script src="../lib/meditate-temperature.js"></script>

<meditate-temperature heat="0.38" breath="0.5" glow="0.58"></meditate-temperature>

<script>
  const v = document.querySelector('meditate-temperature');
  v.loadMidi('/audio/score.mid').then(console.log);
  v.setTemp({ heat: 0.8 });
</script>
