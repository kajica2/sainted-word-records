Animated Dingbat Stack

SVG pattern layers · noise displacement

Twelve stroke-based ornament glyphs laid out as a full-frame contact sheet — a 9×3 grid of rows across the video canvas with margin spacing between cells (flip to fewer rows / more columns, or to stacked pattern layers). The whole sheet rolls through the glyph series one step at a time (x+1), while an SVG feTurbulence → feDisplacementMap pipeline displaces the sheet under evolving noise, so the pattern morphs and never repeats. Pick a glyph, change the rows/columns, push the displacement.
